THE CALIBRATION LOOP
04 / 05

Turn Prompt "Vibes"
Into Explicit Policy

Failing boundary cases force product and engineering teams to resolve ambiguity once:

1.Catch the drift in CI on stratified edge-case tickets.
2.Decide the policy explicitly (e.g., phishing questions route to support, not on-call).
3.Lock it into few-shot rubrics so future prompt edits never regress.
